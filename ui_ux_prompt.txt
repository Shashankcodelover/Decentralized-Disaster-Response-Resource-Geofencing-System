## Generator Prompt Specification

Build a premium, responsive, and visually delightful web interface for "Project Mirage — Decentralized Disaster Response System" utilizing a premium, modern Light Theme. The interface must include a dynamic initial Splash Screen Gateway that splits into three distinct role-based sub-systems (HQ Commander/Admin, Field Responder, and Logistics Coordinator).

### 1. AESTHETICS & EMOTIONAL SYSTEM THEME
- **Visual Feel:** "Tactical Clarity and High Visibility." Designed to look like a clean, advanced tactical operation desk—extremely professional, reliable, and legible under high stress. Soft light grays, warm charcoal headers, and intense warning accents ensure absolute data readability under any lighting condition.
- **Color Palette (Light Glassmorphic):**
  * Canvas Base Background: Light Slate Grey (#f1f5f9)
  * Panel Backgrounds: Milky Translucent Acrylic (rgba(248, 250, 252, 0.8))
  * Borders: 1px thin outlines with tactical linear gradients (linear-gradient(135deg, rgba(234, 88, 12, 0.2), rgba(34, 197, 94, 0.15)))
  * Primary Alert Accent: Fire Orange (#ea580c)
  * Secondary P2P Accent: Acid Green (#22c55e)
  * Highlight Warning: Hazard Yellow (#eab308)
  * Muted Details: Soft Slate Grey (#64748b)
- **Filters & Shadows:** `backdrop-filter: blur(14px)`, box-shadow: `0 8px 30px rgba(100, 116, 139, 0.08)`.
- **Typography:** Space Grotesk (tactical bold headings), JetBrains Mono (real-time coordinates, terminals, and CRDT tables).
